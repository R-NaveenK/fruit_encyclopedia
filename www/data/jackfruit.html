Short Description

Jackfruit is the largest fruit grown on trees and is native to tropical regions. When ripe, it is sweet and aromatic; when unripe, it is used as a vegetable. Jackfruit is rich in nutrients and widely used in traditional cooking.

Nutritional Value

Nutrient Amount
Calories 95 kcal
Carbohydrates 23 g
Dietary Fiber 1.5 g
Vitamin C Present
Potassium 448 mg
Vitamin A 23%

Health Benefits

  • Boosts immunity with vitamin C
  • Provides long-lasting energy
  • Improves digestion
  • Supports heart health
  • Improves skin and hair health

Best Time to Eat

Jackfruit is best eaten in the morning or early afternoon.It is heavy to digest, so eating it early allows proper digestion.

Avoid eating jackfruit:

  • With dairy products
  • Late at night

Best practice: Small portions + well-ripened fruit

Who Should Avoid

  • Diabetic patients, due to sugar content
  • People with digestion problems
  • Those allergic to jackfruit latex

Fun Fact

  • Jackfruit is the largest tree-grown fruit in the world
  • Jackfruit seeds are edible and nutritious
  • Unripe jackfruit is used as a meat substitute
