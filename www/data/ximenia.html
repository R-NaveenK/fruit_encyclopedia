Short Description

Ximenia is a wild fruit commonly found in Africa and parts of Asia. It has a sour taste and is used traditionally in local medicine and food.

Nutritional Value

Nutrient Amount
Calories 45 kcal
Carbohydrates 10 g
Fiber Moderate
Vitamin C Present
Antioxidants Present

Health Benefits

  • Supports digestive health
  • Provides antioxidants
  • Used in traditional medicine
  • Boosts immunity

Best Time to Eat

Best eaten:

  • During daytime
  • After proper ripening

Avoid eating raw or unripe fruit.

Who Should Avoid

  • People with sensitive stomachs
  • Those unfamiliar with wild fruits
  • Children, without supervision

Fun Fact

  • Ximenia is also called Sour Plum
  • Grows naturally in dry regions
  • Used in traditional African healing
