Short Description

Tangerine is a citrus fruit similar to orange but smaller and sweeter. It is easy to peel and widely consumed fresh.

Nutritional Value

Nutrient Amount
Calories 53 kcal
Carbohydrates 13 g
Dietary Fiber 1.8 g
Vitamin C 44%
Potassium 166 mg
Antioxidants Present

Health Benefits

  • Strengthens immune system
  • Improves skin health
  • Supports digestion
  • Maintains heart health
  • Provides hydration

Best Time to Eat

Tangerines are best eaten:

  • Morning or afternoon
  • As a refreshing snack

Avoid eating:

  • At night
  • On empty stomach if acidic

Who Should Avoid

  • People with acid reflux
  • Those with sensitive teeth
  • People with cold, if eaten at night

Fun Fact

  • Tangerines are named after Tangier, Morocco
  • They are sweeter than oranges
  • Tangerines peel easily due to loose skin
