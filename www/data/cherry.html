Short Description

Cherry is a small, round stone fruit that can be sweet or sour. It is commonly grown in cooler climates and is widely used in desserts, juices, and jams. Cherries are known for their bright red color and refreshing taste.

Nutritional Value

Nutrient Amount
Calories 50 kcal
Carbohydrates 12 g

Potassium 173 mg
Vitamin C 10%
Antioxidants High

Health Benefits

  • Improves sleep quality due to melatonin
  • Reduces inflammation and joint pain
  • Supports heart health
  • Helps muscle recovery
  • Boosts immunity

Best Time to Eat

Cherries are best eaten in the evening or night, especially after dinner. They help relax the body and improve sleep quality.

Avoid overeating cherries because:

  • Excess fiber may cause gas
  • Sour varieties may increase acidity

Best practice: Small portion + fresh cherries.

Who Should Avoid

  • People with acid reflux, especially sour cherries
  • Those with sensitive digestion
  • People allergic to stone fruits

Fun Fact

  • Cherries naturally contain melatonin
  • Cherry trees can live for 20–30 years
  • Athletes consume cherries for muscle recovery
