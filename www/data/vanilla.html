Short Description

Vanilla comes from the fruit pod of the vanilla orchid. Although mainly used as a flavoring agent, the pod itself is considered a fruit. It has a sweet aroma and is widely used in desserts.

Nutritional Value

Nutrient Amount
Calories 288 kcal
Carbohydrates 13 g
Calcium 11 mg
Potassium 148 mg
Sugars Low
Antioxidants Present

Health Benefits

  • Improves mood and reduces stress
  • Contains antioxidant properties
  • Supports digestive health
  • May reduce inflammation

Best Time to Eat

Vanilla is best consumed:

  • In small quantities
  • During daytime
  • Mixed with other foods

Avoid eating:

  • At night

Who Should Avoid

  • People with allergies to vanilla
  • Those with migraines, if sensitive to aromas
  • Pregnant women, in excess

Fun Fact

  • Vanilla is the second most expensive spice
  • Vanilla pods must be hand-pollinated
  • One pod takes 9 months to grow
